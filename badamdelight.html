Badam Delight

A chilled badam-based drink with a rich, nutty taste and smooth finish.

Signature Drink

What you get

  • Badam base Soaked and blended for thickness
  • Sweetener Sugar or honey (customisable)
  • Flavor touch Cardamom or saffron (optional)
  • Serving style Chilled cup, perfect post-class refresh

Badam Delight is prepared using soaked badam that is blended for a smooth, rich texture. It is chilled properly and served cold for a creamy, nutty taste in every sip. The drink is balanced to feel filling and refreshing, perfect for a quick energy boost on a warm day.

The drink is gently chilled and lightly sweetened to maintain perfect balance without overpowering the natural taste of almonds. Every sip delivers warmth, nourishment, and freshness together. Badam Delight is ideal for those who enjoy classic flavours with a refreshing twist.

Best for: quick energy, rich taste, and people who want a classic chilled drink.
